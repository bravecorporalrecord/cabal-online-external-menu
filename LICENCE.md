## 🧩 About

**Cabal Online External Menu** is a curated list of Cabal Online open-source tools:

- **Overlays** — on-screen helpers, layout notes, hitbox viewer, coordinate tracker
- **Quality-of-Life Helpers** — inventory catalog, skill manager, overlay notes
- **Research Notes** — dungeon layouts and config notes for educational use

Based on publicly available community tools and overlay helpers.

## ✨ Features

### ESP
- Coordinate Tracker — record and review movement paths
- Hitbox Viewer — visualize attack ranges and collision boxes
- Skill Timer Notes — track cooldowns and buff durations
- Playback Speed — adjust training dummy speed for study (0.1x to 5x)

### Wallhack
- Item Catalog — browse equipment, costumes, and dyes
- Custom Skin Notes — organize character appearances
- Glow Trail — improved visual feedback for effects

### Aimbot
- Dungeon Practice Notes — segment study on any map
- Camera Control — manually adjust zoom and angle mid-fight
- Combo Buffer Notes — chain complex skill rotations in training
